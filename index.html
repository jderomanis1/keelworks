How it works

Brief → crew → gates → ship

  1. 01

    Brief

    You state the outcome, constraints, and non-negotiables. Short is fine; vague is not.

  2. 02

    Crew

    Specialist AI hands draft, implement, and iterate inside the brief — fast, parallel, accountable to the frame.

  3. 03

    Human gates

    You review judgment, taste, and risk. Green light, redirect, or kill. The keel decides.

  4. 04

    Ship

    We deliver the artifact — site, app, tool, or slice — ready to use, not a slide about using it.
