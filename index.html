Good questions

No mystery.
No magic claims.

Do I need to understand AI or technology?

No. Bring the idea, the problem, or even the task that keeps frustrating you. Joe will help turn it into a clear next step and explain the tradeoffs without burying you in technical detail.

Can you really build anything?

We can explore a very wide range of digital ideas. That isn’t a promise that every idea is feasible, safe, or sensible at every budget. We’ll pressure-test the hard parts early and be direct about limits, dependencies, and costs.

Is the Possibility Studio a live AI chatbot?

No. It’s an interactive, browser-only brief builder and a transparent calculator. The suggestions come from your selected project type and priorities, not a language model interpreting your idea. Your original words are carried into an editable brief. There are no AI API calls or model downloads in these demos.

What does a project cost?

That depends on what we’re building, what it needs to connect to, and how it will be used. We start with a conversation, define a useful first version, and agree the scope and cost before committing. We also separate build costs from hosting, subscriptions, and ongoing support.

What about privacy, ownership, and human oversight?

Those are part of the scope, not an afterthought. Before connecting systems or introducing AI, we agree on data access, approvals, ownership, and support. We won’t assume confidential information belongs in an AI tool, or that an AI should take consequential actions without human review.
